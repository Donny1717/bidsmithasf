## 📋 **Executive Summary**

**Guidance from the Cabinet Office** on managing risks and opportunities associated with Artificial Intelligence (AI) in public procurement. This PPN clarifies that suppliers' use of AI in tender responses is **not prohibited**, provided risks are managed and disclosed via Annex B questions, while mandating strict controls to prevent confidential government data from being used to train AI models.

### 🎯 **Key Objectives:**
- Establish clear guidelines for AI usage in procurement processes
- Ensure transparency and accountability in AI-assisted bidding
- Protect confidential government information from AI model training
- Maintain fair competition while allowing technological advancement

## ⚖️ **Key Obligations & Requirements**

### 📌 **For Contracting Authorities:**

1. **AI Disclosure Questions**
   - ✅ **Requirement:** Include Annex B questions in tender documents to disclose AI usage
   - 📝 **Implementation:** Add specific questions about AI tools used in bid preparation
   - ⚠️ **Purpose:** For information and due diligence purposes only (NOT scored)

2. **Confidential Data Protection**
   - 🔒 **Requirement:** Mandate controls to prevent use of confidential information for AI training
   - 🛡️ **Implementation:** Include contractual clauses prohibiting AI model training with government data
   - 📊 **Verification:** Conduct audits to ensure compliance

3. **Due Diligence**
   - 🔍 **Requirement:** Perform proportionate due diligence on AI-generated content
   - 📋 **Methods:** Site visits, clarification requests, presentations
   - 🎯 **Objective:** Verify accuracy and credibility against LLM "hallucination" risks

4. **National Security Considerations**
   - 🛡️ **Requirement:** Special engagement for AI in national security procurements
   - 🤝 **Action:** Consult with Information Assurance and Security colleagues
   - ⏰ **Timing:** Prior to launching procurement

### 📌 **For Suppliers/Bidders:**

1. **AI Usage Permission**
   - ✅ **Allowed:** Use of AI tools to draft tender responses
   - ⚠️ **Condition:** Human verification against hallucinations required
   - 📝 **Disclosure:** Must disclose AI usage when requested by authorities

2. **Data Protection**
   - 🔒 **Requirement:** Strictly protect client/government confidential data
   - 🚫 **Prohibition:** Confidential data MUST NOT be used in AI training sets
   - 📊 **Audit:** Maintain audit trails of AI tool usage

3. **Service Delivery Integration**
   - 🤖 **Requirement:** Declare AI integration details under Example Disclosure Question 3
   - 📋 **Scope:** When AI is integrated into service delivery for non-AI services

## 📎 **Annex A: Example AI Disclosure Questions**

### **Annex B Question 1: AI Usage Declaration**

> **Question:** Has your organization used Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools, including but not limited to Large Language Models (LLMs), in the preparation of this tender response?
>
> **Answer Options:**
> - [ ] Yes, AI tools were used
> - [ ] No, no AI tools were used
> - [ ] Unsure

> **If Yes:**
> - Please specify which AI tools were used: _______________
> - For what purposes were AI tools used: _______________
> - What percentage of the tender response was AI-generated: ____%

### **Annex B Question 2: Data Protection Confirmation**

> **Question:** Do you confirm that no confidential government information or non-public tender documents were used as training data for any AI models in the preparation of this bid?
>
> **Answer Options:**
> - [ ] Yes, I confirm
> - [ ] No, confidential data may have been used
> - [ ] Unsure

### **Annex B Question 3: AI Integration in Service Delivery**

> **Question:** Will your proposed solution integrate AI technologies in the delivery of services under this contract?
>
> **Answer Options:**
> - [ ] Yes, AI will be integrated
> - [ ] No, AI will not be integrated
> - [ ] Possibly in future phases

> **If Yes:**
> - Please describe the AI technologies to be used: _______________
> - How will AI enhance service delivery: _______________
> - What data will the AI systems process: _______________

## 📎 **Annex B: Due Diligence Checklist**

### **For Contracting Authorities:**

| **Check** | **Description** | **Completed** |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| [ ] | Verify AI disclosure responses | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Assess plausibility of AI-generated content | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Check for consistency across bid documents | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Validate technical accuracy of AI-assisted sections | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Confirm data protection measures are in place | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Review audit trails for AI usage | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Conduct site visit if AI usage is significant | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Request clarification on AI-related aspects | ⬜ |
| [ ] | Document verification findings | ⬜ |

### **Verification Methods:**

1. **Content Analysis:**
   - Check for AI-generated patterns (repetitive phrasing, unusual formatting)
   - Verify technical accuracy and specificity
   - Assess depth of domain knowledge demonstrated

2. **Consistency Review:**
   - Cross-reference AI-generated sections with other bid documents
   - Check for internal consistency in technical specifications
   - Verify alignment with stated capabilities and experience

3. **Technical Validation:**
   - Request evidence of human verification processes
   - Review quality assurance procedures for AI-generated content
   - Assess technical competence in AI-related aspects

## 🔍 **Risk Assessment**

### **Identified Risks:**

| **Risk** | **Likelihood** | **Impact** | **Risk Level** | **Mitigation** |
|----------|---------------|------------|----------------|----------------|
| AI Hallucination | Medium | High | 🔴 **High** | Human verification, due diligence |
| Data Leakage | Low | Critical | 🔴 **High** | Strict data protection controls |
| Bias in AI Output | Medium | Medium | 🟡 **Medium** | Diverse training data, human review |
| Over-reliance on AI | Medium | Medium | 🟡 **Medium** | Clear guidelines, training |
| Non-compliance | Low | High | 🟡 **Medium** | Monitoring, audits |

### **Risk Mitigation Strategies:**

1. **For AI Hallucination Risk:**
   - Require human verification of all AI-generated content
   - Implement multi-stage review processes
   - Conduct technical accuracy checks
   - Request supporting evidence and documentation

2. **For Data Leakage Risk:**
   - Mandate strict data protection clauses in contracts
   - Require confidentiality agreements for AI tool providers
   - Implement data access controls and monitoring
   - Conduct regular security audits

3. **For Bias Risk:**
   - Require disclosure of AI training data sources
   - Implement bias detection processes
   - Conduct diversity and inclusion reviews
   - Require fairness testing of AI systems
